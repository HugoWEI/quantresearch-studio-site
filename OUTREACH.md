# Customer acquisition sprint — first 20 prospects

## Single goal
Discover whether quant and research applicants have a real need for the proposed **$24 one-time bundle**. Avoid spending on ads or writing more templates until we understand their objections.

## Offer and links
- Landing page: https://hugowei.github.io/quantresearch-studio-site/
- Free source sample: https://hugowei.github.io/quantresearch-studio-site/free-sample/quant-cv.tex
- Public feedback: https://github.com/HugoWEI/quantresearch-studio-site/issues/1
- Status: prelaunch, no checkout, no payments or conversion tracking yet.
- This public GitHub thread requires login; its replies are public. It is an interim way to collect feedback, not a representative survey.

## 1:1 outreach — ask before sending long pitches
Message A (quant applicant):
> I'm testing a small LaTeX document kit for quant applicants. The first version has a free editable quant CV sample, and I'm considering a $24 bundle with research templates. Would you be willing to look at the sample for 60 seconds and tell me whether you'd actually pay for something like this? No purchase or signup required: https://hugowei.github.io/quantresearch-studio-site/

Message B (PhD applicant / researcher):
> I'm testing whether researchers would pay for a polished LaTeX bundle (academic CV, conference poster and research-statement template) instead of using free templates. There's a preview and free quant CV sample here: https://hugowei.github.io/quantresearch-studio-site/. What would make a research document toolkit useful to you?

Do not mass-message strangers, scrape contact details or misrepresent affiliation. Seek opt-in conversations and comply with communities' self-promotion rules.

## 15-minute interview prompts
1. Show me the CV, poster or statement you currently edit (only if they're comfortable; don't request confidential materials).
2. What exactly consumes the most time?
3. Which free template have you tried, and what was missing?
4. Have you ever paid for an academic/job application template? How much?
5. Would you buy **this bundle at $24** today? Why or why not?
6. If not, is the obstacle price, credibility, content fit, or absence of Word support?

## Decision criteria
- 20 thoughtful conversations is a discovery milestone, not demand validation.
- 5 completed $24 purchases from qualified buyers would be encouraging early evidence, **after** sales and payment setup.
- 'Would buy' is hypothetical intent. An email waitlist or GitHub issue comment is not a sale.
- If the most common request is a quant CV alone, test an unbundled offer before creating more templates.
