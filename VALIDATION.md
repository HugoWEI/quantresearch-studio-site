# QuantResearch Studio — 7-day customer-validation sprint

**Offer:** Proposed $24 one-time LaTeX bundle (quant CV, academic research CV, poster and PhD research statement). **Status:** Prelaunch; no sales or customer data collection in this repository.

## Audience
Prioritize students and early-career quantitative finance applicants. Research applicants are the secondary segment. Do not present friendly compliments as evidence of purchasing.

## Experiment
1. Send a customized preview link to 20 **consenting/appropriate** contacts. Avoid bulk unsolicited messages and follow each community's promotional rules.
2. For each person, ask what they currently use, whether they have paid for a CV or poster template, what is most frustrating, and what part of this specific bundle they would buy.
3. Get 200 relevant landing-page visitors through permitted distribution channels; track visitor totals by channel once privacy-conscious analytics are configured.
4. Offer the free editable LaTeX sample. Collect survey responses only after publishing consent/privacy information.
5. After the legal/payment setup is ready, test **real $24 checkout**, clearly stating delivery terms and refund policy.

## Questions for a 90-second survey
- Which best describes you? (Quant trading/QR applicant; quant dev applicant; academic researcher; Physics/ML PhD applicant; other)
- Which document is your most immediate need? (Quant CV / research CV / poster / research statement / none)
- Which format are you using now? (LaTeX / Word or Docs / Canva / other)
- Have you ever paid for a document template? (Yes; No)
- How likely would you be to buy this *specific* $24 bundle? (Definitely / Probably / Unsure / Probably not / Definitely not)
- What would need to change for you to consider buying? (free text)

**Optional contact field:** email to notify about launch, with separate explicit opt-in and privacy notice. Keep answers private; do not publish names or addresses.

## Metrics
- Unique qualified visitors (once consent-respecting analytics are live)
- Free sample downloads (when tracking is added)
- Survey starts/completions
- Paid orders, refunds, channel attribution and revenue (once checkout is active)
- Weekly support hours

## Decision gates
- Five $24 *completed purchases* from genuinely relevant customers: encouraging, but not proof of sustainable demand.
- Strong CV-only preference: consider splitting or repositioning the product.
- High interest but no buyers: revisit promise, product demonstration, trust and price.
- Low qualified traffic: do not mistake this for low demand; improve distribution first.

## What is NOT a sale
Email signup, page visit, sample download, survey response, verbal pledge or an abandoned checkout.

## Site administration
The repository is public. Never commit private paid .tex source files, API keys, payment credentials, survey responses or customer information.
