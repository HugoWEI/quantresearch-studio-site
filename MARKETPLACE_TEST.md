# Marketplace-first validation (no personal network)

**Status:** internal launch draft; do **not** publish a paid listing until all promised deliverables, seller/account obligations and checkout are ready. No orders have been recorded.

## Why marketplaces first?
Unlike emailing friends or waiting for a new GitHub Pages site to rank, a marketplace offers an existing product-search interface. This *does not guarantee* visitors or sales. Use a small, measured listing test instead of mass cold outreach.

## Primary experiment — Etsy
- Product type: original, seller-designed digital download (ZIP with editable LaTeX source plus compiled previews and instructions).
- Proposed price: **$24** (local store currency display may differ).
- Proposed title (12 words): **Quant Finance LaTeX CV and Research Template Bundle | Editable Files**
- Do not claim recruiter endorsement, admissions guarantees, ATS certification, or an association with any institution.
- No paid ads in the first experiment.

### Description draft (for after product QA)
**LaTeX documents for technical careers and research.**

An editable document kit for students and early-career professionals applying to quantitative finance roles and research programs. The goal is to help you explain your actual work clearly and spend less time on formatting.

**Planned contents (verify before listing):**
- Quant finance CV: concise experience-first document.
- Academic research CV: project- and publication-friendly structure.
- Research poster: scientific conference layout.
- PhD research statement: structured presentation of research questions, methods, contributions, limitations and next steps.
- Fictional examples, setup instructions and compilation checklist.

**Format:** downloadable ZIP containing source `.tex` files, PDF previews and documentation. Users edit in a LaTeX environment such as Overleaf or a local TeX installation. No Word template is included.

**Important:** These are templates, not a CV-writing service. The user must replace all fictional examples with truthful information. No interviews, admissions, hiring outcomes or ATS passage are guaranteed. This is a digital download, not a physical product.

**Questions before buying?** Check that you're comfortable editing LaTeX and that the example layouts fit your needs.

## Avoid misleading distribution ideas
- Overleaf expressly excludes CV / resume templates from its Gallery and rejects promotional material: https://docs.overleaf.com/templates/submitting-to-the-overleaf-template-gallery
- Show HN is for nontrivial projects people can actually try, not just a landing page or promotional page: https://news.ycombinator.com/showhn.html
- Respect individual subreddit self-promotion rules. Do not spam CV advice threads with sales links.

## Validation metrics
*Only count actual platform analytics*, and separate source visits, listing views, purchases, refunded orders, and customer support time.
- 0 sales with fewer than ~100 relevant listing views: insufficient to conclude lack of demand.
- 0 sales with several hundred relevant listing views: examine offer, title, price and previews critically.
- 3+ full-price sales: encouraging direction, not evidence of sustainable $100 monthly profit.
- Track contribution margins after fees, refunds, VAT and personal tax scenarios.
- Stop immediately if support time or paid acquisition costs make the low-maintenance goal unrealistic.
